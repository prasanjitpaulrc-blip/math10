Exercise 11

1. Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius \(6 \;cm\) if angle of the sector is \(60^\circ\).

Solution:

\(\;\;\; Given,\)

\(\qquad Radius\;of\;the\;circle,\;r=6\;cm\)

\(\qquad Angle\;of\;the\;sector,\;\theta=60^\circ\)

\(\quad \therefore\;Area\;of\;the\;sector=\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\pi r^2\)


\(\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\quad\; =\dfrac{60^\circ}{360^\circ}\times\dfrac{22}{7}\times6^2 \)


\(\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\quad\; =\dfrac{1}{6}\times\dfrac{22}{7}\times6 \times6 \)


\(\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad\quad\; =\dfrac{132}{7}\;cm^2 \)

2. Find the area of a quadrant of a circle whose circumference is \(22 \;cm\).

Solution: 2πr=22 → r=22/(2π)=22×7/(2×22)=3.5 cm. Quadrant area = (1/4)πr² = (1/4)×(22/7)×12.25 = 9.625 cm².

3. The length of the minute hand of a clock is \(14 \;cm\). Find the area swept by the minute hand in \(5\) minutes.

Solution: In 60 min, angle = 360°. In 5 min, angle = 30°. Area swept = (30°/360°)×π×14² = (1/12)×(22/7)×196 = (1/12)×616 = 154/3 ≈ 51.33 cm².

4. A chord of a circle of radius \(10\; cm\) subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding: \((i)\) \(minor\) \(segment\) \((ii)\) \(major \;sector\). \((Use \;π=3.14)\)

Solution: (i) Area of sector = (90/360)×π×10² = (1/4)×3.14×100 = 78.5 cm². Area of triangle = (1/2)×10×10=50 cm². Minor segment area = 78.5−50=28.5 cm². (ii) Major sector area = πr² − minor sector = 314 − 78.5 = 235.5 cm².

5. In a circle of radius \(21\; cm,\) an arc subtends an angle of \(60^\circ\) at the centre. Find:
\(\;(i)\) \(length\) \(of\) \(the\) \(arc\)
\(\;(ii)\) \(area\) \(of\) \(the\) \(sector\) \(formed\) \(by\) \(the\) \(arc\)
\(\;(iii)\) \(area\) \(of\) \(the\) \(segment\) \(formed\) \(by\) \(the\) \(corresponding\) \(chord\).

Solution: (i) Arc length = (60/360)×2π×21 = (1/6)×2×(22/7)×21 = 22 cm. (ii) Sector area = (60/360)×π×21² = (1/6)×(22/7)×441 = 231 cm². (iii) Triangle area (equilateral) = (√3/4)×21² = 441√3/4 ≈ 190.95 cm². Segment area = 231 − 190.95 = 40.05 cm².

6. A chord of a circle of radius \(15 \;cm\) subtends an angle of \(60^\circ\) at the centre. Find the areas of the corresponding minor and major segments. \((Use \;\pi=3.14 \;and \;\sqrt{3}=1.73)\)

Solution: Sector area = (60/360)×3.14×225 = 117.75 cm². Triangle area = (1/2)×15×15×sin60° = (225×√3)/4 = 225×1.73/4 = 97.3125 cm². Minor segment = 117.75−97.31=20.44 cm². Major segment = πr² − minor segment = 706.5−20.44=686.06 cm².

7. A chord of a circle of radius \(12 \;cm\) subtends an angle of \(120^\circ\) at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding segment of the circle. \((Use \pi=3.14,\; \sqrt{3}=1.73)\)

Solution: Sector area = (120/360)×3.14×144 = (1/3)×452.16 = 150.72 cm². Triangle area: drop altitude, height = 12×cos60°=6, base = 2×12×sin60°=12√3 ≈20.76 cm. Area = (1/2)×20.76×6 = 62.28 cm². Segment area = 150.72−62.28=88.44 cm².

8. A horse is tied to a peg at one corner of a square shaped grass field of side \(15 \;m\) by means of a \(5\; m\) long rope \((see\;Fig.\;11.8)\). Find
\(\;(i)\) \(the \;area\; of \;that \;part\; of\) \(the\) \(field\) \(in\) \(which\) \(the\) \(horse\) \(can\) \(graze\).
\(\;(ii)\) \(the \;increase \;in\; the\; grazing \;area\) \(if\) \(the\) \(rope\) \(were\) \(10\; m\) \(long\) \(instead\) \(of\) \(5\; m\). \((Use\; \pi=3.14)\)

Solution: (i) Grazing area = quadrant of radius 5 m = (1/4)×3.14×25 = 19.625 m². (ii) New quadrant radius 10 m: area = (1/4)×3.14×100=78.5 m². Increase = 78.5−19.625=58.875 m².

9. A brooch is made with silver wire in the form of a circle with diameter \(35\; mm\). The wire is also used in making \(5\) diameters which divide the circle into \(10\) equal sectors as shown in \(Fig.\;11.9\). Find:
\(\;(i)\) \(the \;total \;length \;of\; the\; silver\) \(wire\) \(required\).
\(\;(ii)\) \(the\; area \;of \;each \;sector\; of\) \(the\) \(brooch\).

Solution: Radius = 17.5 mm. Circumference = 2π×17.5 = 110 mm. Total length of 5 diameters = 5×35 = 175 mm. Total wire = 110+175 = 285 mm. (ii) Each sector angle = 36°. Area = (36/360)×π×(17.5)² = (1/10)×(22/7)×306.25 = 96.25 mm².

10. An umbrella has \(8\) ribs which are equally spaced \((see\;Fig.11.10)\). Assuming umbrella to be a flat circle of radius \(45\; cm,\) find the area between the two consecutive ribs of the umbrella.

Solution: Angle between ribs = 360°/8 = 45°. Area between ribs = (45/360)×π×45² = (1/8)×(22/7)×2025 = 5568.75/7 ≈ 795.54 cm².

11. A car has two wipers which do not overlap. Each wiper has a blade of length \(25\; cm\) sweeping through an angle of \(115^\circ\). Find the total area cleaned at each sweep of the blades.

Solution: Area swept by one blade = (115/360)×π×25² = (23/72)×(22/7)×625 ≈ 1584.82 cm². Total for two blades = 2 × 1584.82 ≈ 3169.64 cm².

12. To warn ships for underwater rocks, a lighthouse spreads a red coloured light over a sector of angle \(80^\circ\) to a distance of \(16.5\; km\). Find the area of the sea over which the ships are warned. \((Use \;π=3.14)\)

Solution: Area = (80/360)×3.14×(16.5)² = (2/9)×3.14×272.25 ≈ 189.97 km².

13. A round table cover has six equal designs as shown in \(fig.\;11.11\). If the radius of the cover is \(28 \;cm,\) find the cost of making the designs at the rate of \(Rs.0.35\; per \;cm^2\). \((Use \;\sqrt{3}=1.7)\)

Solution: Six designs correspond to 6 segments of a circle. Each segment angle = 60°. Sector area = (60/360)×π×28² = (1/6)×(22/7)×784 = 410.67 cm². Triangle area = (√3/4)×28² = (1.7/4)×784 = 333.2 cm². Segment area = 410.67−333.2=77.47 cm² per segment. Total area for 6 segments = 6×77.47=464.82 cm². Cost = 464.82×0.35 = ₹162.69.

NCERT Class 10 Math Chapter 12 – Areas Related to Circles FAQs

Clear conceptual answers to the most common queries from Areas Related to Circles.

What is the formula for the circumference of a circle?

Circumference = 2πr or πd, where r is radius and d is diameter.

How is the area of a circle calculated?

Area = πr². Remember π is approximately 22/7 or 3.14.

What is the area of a sector of a circle?

Area of sector = \( \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2 \), where \( \theta \) is the central angle in degrees.

How do you find the area of a segment of a circle?

Area of minor segment = area of sector – area of corresponding triangle. For a major segment, subtract minor segment area from total circle area.

What is the relationship between circumference and diameter?

Circumference = π × diameter. The constant π (pi) is the ratio of circumference to diameter of any circle.

How to find the radius from the area of a circle?

r = √(Area/π). For example, if area is 154 cm², r = √(154×7/22) = 7 cm.

What is a quadrant of a circle?

A quadrant is one‑fourth of a circle, with a central angle of 90°. Its area is \( \frac{1}{4} \pi r^2 \).

How do you calculate the area of a circular ring (annulus)?

Area = \( \pi (R^2 - r^2) \), where R and r are outer and inner radii.

What is the perimeter of a sector?

Perimeter of a sector = 2r + arc length, where arc length = \( \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r \).

How to find the area of a combination of plane figures involving circles?

Break down the shape into known components (rectangles, triangles, semicircles, sectors, etc.), calculate their areas, and then add or subtract as needed for the shaded region.
