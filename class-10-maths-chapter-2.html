Exercise 2.1

1. The graphs of y = p(x) are given below, for some polynomials p(x). Find the number of zeroes of p(x), in each case.

Solution: Number of zeroes of a polynomial \(p(x)\) = number of points where the graph of \( y=p(x) \) cuts or touches the \(X\)-axis.


\(\;(i)\) The graph does not intersect the \(X\)-axis.
\( \therefore\; Number\; of\; zeroes=0 \)


\(\;(ii)\) The graph intersects the \(X\)-axis at one point.
\( \therefore \;Number \;of\; zeroes=1 \)


\(\;(iii)\) The graph intersects the \(X\)-axis at three points.
\( \therefore \;Number \;of\; zeroes=3 \)


\(\;(iv)\) The graph intersects the \(X\)-axis at two points.
\( \therefore \;Number\; of\; zeroes=2 \)


\(\;(v)\) The graph intersects the \(X\)-axis at four points.
\( \therefore \;Number \;of \;zeroes=4 \)


\(\;(vi)\) The graph intersects/touches the \(X\)-axis at three points.
\( \therefore \;Number \;of \;zeroes=3 \)

Exercise 2.2

Find the zeroes of the following polynomials and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

\(\text{(i)}\ x^2 - 2x - 8 \) \(\quad\text{(ii)}\ 4s^2 - 4s + 1 \)
\(\text{(iii)}\ 6x^2 - 3- 7x \) \(\quad \text{(iv)}\ 4u^2 + 8u \)
\(\text{(v)}\ t^2 - 15 \) \(\qquad \text{(vi)}\ 3x^2 - x - 4 \)
\( \text{(vii)}\ x^2 + 7x + 12 \)
\( \text{(viii)}\ x^2 - 4x + 3 \) \( \quad\text{(ix)}\ x^2 - 6x - 7 \)
\( \text{(x)}\ 2x^2 - 5x - 7 \)

Solution:

\((i)\)\(Let\),

\(\quad\quad p(x)=x^{2}-2x-8 \)
\(\qquad\quad =x^{2}-4x+2x-8 \)
\(\qquad\quad =x(x-4)+2(x-4) \)
\(\qquad\quad =(x-4)(x+2) \)

\(\;So, \;the \;value \;of \;p(x) \;is \;zero\)

\(\;When,\)
\(\qquad x-4=0 \;\;\; \text{or} \;\;\; x+2=0 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow x=4\qquad\text{or} \qquad x=-2 \)

\(\therefore\) \(Required\) \(zeroes\) \(of\) \(the\) \(p(x)\) \(are\) \(4\) \(and\) \(-2\).

\(Verification:\)

\(\quad \alpha+\beta= \) \( 4+(-2)=2 \)

\(\quad \dfrac{-b}{a} = \dfrac{-(-2)}{1} =2 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(and\)
\(\quad \alpha \times \beta\) \( =4\times(-2)=-8 \)

\(\qquad \dfrac{c}{a} =\dfrac{-8}{1} =-8 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(\; Hence, \;the\) \(relationship\) \(between\) \(zeroes\) \(and\) \(coefficients\) \(is\) \(verified\).



\((ii)\)\(Let\),

\(\quad\quad p(s)=4s^{2}-4s+1 \)
\(\qquad\quad =4s^{2}-2s-2s+1 \)
\(\qquad\quad =2s(2s-1)-1(2s-1) \)
\(\qquad\quad =(2s-1)(2s-1) \)

\(\;So, \;the \;value \;of \;p(s) \;is \;zero\)

\(\;When,\)
\(\qquad 2s-1=0\quad\text{or}\quad 2s-1=0 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow 2s=1\qquad\text{or}\qquad 2s=1 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow s=\dfrac12\qquad\text{or}\qquad s=\dfrac12 \)

\(\therefore\) \(Required\) \(zeroes\) \(of\) \(the\) \(p(s)\) \(are\) \(\dfrac12\) \(and\) \(\dfrac12\).

\(Verification:\)

\(\quad \alpha+\beta= \) \( \dfrac12+\dfrac12=1 \)

\(\quad \dfrac{-b}{a} = \dfrac{-(-4)}{4} =1 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(and\)
\(\quad \alpha \times \beta \) \( =\dfrac12\times\dfrac12 =\dfrac14 \)

\(\qquad \dfrac{c}{a} = \dfrac{1}{4} \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(\; Hence, \;the\) \(relationship\) \(between\) \(zeroes\) \(and\) \(coefficients\) \(is\) \(verified\).



\((iii)\)\(Let\),

\(\quad\quad p(x)=6x^{2}-7x-3 \)
\(\qquad\qquad =6x^{2}-9x+2x-3 \)
\(\qquad\qquad =3x(2x-3)+1(2x-3) \)
\(\qquad\qquad =(2x-3)(3x+1) \)

\(\;So, \;the \;value \;of \;p(x) \;is \;zero\)

\(When,\)
\(\qquad 2x-3=0 \quad \text{or} \quad 3x+1=0 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow 2x=3 \qquad \text{or} \qquad 3x=-1 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow x=\dfrac32,\qquad\text{or}\qquad x=\dfrac{-1}{3} \)

\(\therefore\) \(Required\) \(zeroes\) \(of\) \(the\) \(p(x)\) \(are\) \(\dfrac32\) \(and\) \(-\dfrac13\).

\(Verification:\)

\(\quad \alpha+\beta= \) \( \dfrac32+\left(\dfrac{-1}{3}\right) = \dfrac{7}{6} \)

\(\quad \dfrac{-b}{a} = \dfrac{-(-7)}{6} = \dfrac76 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(and\)
\(\quad \alpha \times \beta \) \( =\dfrac32\times\left(\dfrac{-1}{3}\right) =\dfrac{-1}{2} \)

\(\qquad \dfrac{c}{a} = \dfrac{-3}{6} = \dfrac{-1}{2} \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(\; Hence, \;the\) \(relationship\) \(between\) \(zeroes\) \(and\) \(coefficients\) \(is\) \(verified\).



\((iv)\)\(Let\),

\(\qquad\quad p(u)=4u^{2}+8u \)
\(\qquad\qquad\quad =4u(u+2) \)

\(\;So, \;the \;value \;of \;p(u) \;is \;zero\)

\(\;When,\)
\(\qquad\; 4u=0 \quad\;\; \text{or} \quad\;\; u+2=0 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow u=0,\quad\;\;\text{or} \quad\;\; u=-2 \)

\(\therefore\) \(Required\) \(zeroes\) \(of\) \(the\) \(p(u)\) \(are\) \(0\) \(and\) \(-2\).

\(Verification:\)

\(\quad \alpha+\beta= \) \( 0+(-2)=-2 \)

\(\quad \dfrac{-b}{a} =\dfrac{-8}{4} =-2 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(and\)
\(\quad \alpha \times \beta \) \( =0\times(-2)=0 \)

\(\qquad \dfrac{c}{a} = \dfrac{0}{4} =0 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(\; Hence, \;the\) \(relationship\) \(between\) \(zeroes\) \(and\) \(coefficients\) \(is\) \(verified\).

\(\qquad\qquad\;\; =t^{2}-(\sqrt{15})^{2} \)
\(\qquad\qquad\;\; =(t-\sqrt{15})(t+\sqrt{15}) \)

\(Verification:\)

\(\quad \alpha+\beta= \) \( \sqrt{15}+(-\sqrt{15})=0 \)

\(\quad \dfrac{-b}{a} = \dfrac{-0}{1} =0 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(and\)
\(\quad \alpha \times \beta \) \( =\sqrt{15}\times(-\sqrt{15}) =-15 \)

\(\qquad \dfrac{c}{a} = \dfrac{-15}{1} =-15 \)

\(\qquad \therefore\alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(\; Hence, \;the\) \(relationship\) \(between\) \(zeroes\) \(and\) \(coefficients\) \(is\) \(verified\).

2. Find a quadratic polynomial each with the given numbers as the sum and product of its zeroes respectively.

\( \;\;\text{(i)}\ \dfrac{1}{4},\ -1 \) \( \;\text{(ii)}\ \sqrt{2},\ \dfrac{1}{3} \) \( \;\text{(iii)}\ 0,\ \sqrt{5} \)
\( \;\;\text{(iv)}\ 1,\ 1 \) \(\;\; \text{(v)}\ -\dfrac{1}{4},\ \dfrac{1}{4} \) \( \;\text{(vi)}\ 4,\ 1 \)

Solution:

\(\quad(i)\)\(\;Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\quad\; \alpha+\beta=\dfrac14 \)
\(\qquad and \\ \quad\qquad\qquad\; \alpha\beta=-1 \)


\(\;\;So,\; the\; required \;quadratic\; polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form,\)

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =4\left[x^2-\dfrac14x-1\right] \)
\(\qquad\quad =4x^2-x-4, \;\;\; where\; k=4. \)



\(\quad(ii)\)\(\;Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\quad \alpha+\beta=\sqrt2 \)
\(\qquad and \\ \quad\qquad\qquad \alpha\beta=\dfrac13 \)


\(\;\;So, \;the \;required \;quadratic\; polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form,\)

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =3\left[x^2-\sqrt2x+\dfrac13\right] \)
\(\quad\quad\;\; =3x^2-3\sqrt2x+1,\; where\; k=3. \)



\(\quad(iii)\)\(\;Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\quad\; \alpha+\beta=0 \)
\(\qquad and \\ \quad\qquad\qquad \alpha\beta=\sqrt5 \)


\(\;\;So, \;the \;required \;quadratic \;polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form,\)

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =1[x^2-(0)x+\sqrt5] \)
\(\qquad\quad =x^2+\sqrt5,\;\;\; where\; k=1. \)



\(\quad(iv)\)\(\;Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\quad \alpha+\beta=1 \)
\(\qquad and \\ \quad\qquad\qquad \alpha\beta=1 \)


\(\;\;So, \;the \;required \;quadratic\; polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form,\)

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =1[x^2-(1)x+1] \)
\(\qquad\quad =x^2-x+1,\;\;\; where\; k=1. \)



\(\quad(v)\)\(\;Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\quad \alpha+\beta=-\dfrac14 \)
\(\qquad and \\ \quad\qquad\qquad \alpha\beta=\dfrac14 \)


\(\;\;So,\; the \;required\; quadratic \;polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form,\)

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =4\left[x^2-\left(\dfrac{-1}{4}\right)x+\dfrac14\right] \)
\(\qquad\quad =\;4\;\left[x^2+\dfrac{1}{4}x+\dfrac14\right] \)
\(\qquad\quad =4x^2+x+1,\;\;\; where\; k=4. \)



\(\quad(vi)\)\(\;Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\quad \alpha+\beta=4 \)
\(\qquad and \\ \quad\qquad\qquad \alpha\beta=1 \)


\(\;\;So, \;the \;required \;quadratic \;polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form,\)

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =1[x^2-(4)x+1] \)
\(\qquad\quad =x^2-4x+1,\;\;\; where\; k=1. \)

3. Find the quadratic polynomials whose zeroes are -

\( \;\;(i)\ -4 \text{ and } \dfrac{3}{2} \) \(\qquad (ii)\ 5 \text{ and } 2 \)
\( \;\;(iii)\ \dfrac{1}{3} \text{ and } -1 \) \( \quad(iv)\ \dfrac{3}{2} \text{ and } -2 \)

Solution:

\(\;\;(i)\)\(Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad \alpha=-4\;\;and \;\;\beta=\dfrac32\)
\(\;\;\; \therefore\alpha+\beta = -4+\dfrac32 = \dfrac{-5}{2} \)
\(\;and\)
\(\quad\;\; \alpha\beta = (-4)\left(\dfrac32\right) = -6 \)


\(So,\;the\;required\;quadratic\) \(polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form\),

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =2\left[x^2-\left(\dfrac{-5}{2}\right)x+(-6)\right] \)
\(\qquad\quad =\;2\;\left[x^2+\dfrac{5}{2}x-6\right] \)
\(\quad\qquad =2x^2+5x-12,\;\; where\; k=2. \)



\(\;\;(ii)\)\(Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad \alpha=5\;\;and \;\;\beta=2\)
\(\;\;\;\; \therefore\alpha+\beta\; =\; 5+2\; =\; 7 \)
\(\;\;and\)
\(\quad\quad \alpha\beta = 5\times2 = 10 \)


\(So, \;the\; required\; quadratic\) \(polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form\),

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\qquad\quad =1[x^2-(7)x+10] \)
\(\qquad\quad =x^2-7x+10,\; where\; k=1 \)



\(\;\;(iii)\)\(Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad \alpha=\dfrac13\;\;and \;\;\beta=-1\)
\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta = \dfrac13+(-1) = \dfrac{-2}{3} \)
\(\;\;and\)
\(\quad\quad \alpha\beta = \dfrac13\times(-1) = \dfrac{-1}{3} \)


\(So, \;the\; required \;quadratic\) \(polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form\),

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\quad\qquad =3\left[x^2-\left(\dfrac{-2}{3}\right)x+\left(\dfrac{-1}{3}\right)\right] \)
\(\quad\qquad =\;3\;\left[x^2+\dfrac{2}{3}x-\dfrac{1}{3}\right] \)
\(\quad\qquad =3x^2+2x-1,\;\; where\; k=3. \)



\(\;\;(iv)\)\(Let,\)

\(\qquad\qquad \alpha=\dfrac32\;\;and \;\;\beta=-2\)
\(\qquad \therefore\alpha+\beta = \dfrac32+(-2) = \dfrac{-1}{2} \)
\(\;\;and\)
\(\quad\qquad \alpha\beta = \dfrac32\times(-2) = -3 \)


\(So, \;the\;required \;quadratic\) \(polynomial\) \(is\) \(in\) \(the\) \(form\),

\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)

\(\quad\qquad =2\left[x^2-\left(\dfrac{-1}{2}\right)x+(-3)\right] \)

\(\quad\qquad =\;2\;\left[x^2+\dfrac{1}{2}x-3\right] \)

\(\quad\qquad =2x^2+x-6,\;\; where\; k=2. \)

4. If \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial \( x^2 - p(x + 1) + c\), such that \( (\alpha + 1)(\beta + 1) = 0 \) then the value of \( c \) is

 \( (i) \; 1 \quad (ii) \; 2 \quad (iii) \; -1 \quad (iv) \; -2 \)

Solution: \((iii) -1\), is the correct option.


Explanation:

\(\;\;Given ,\)

\(\qquad\quad x^2-p(x+1)+c \)
\(\qquad\Rightarrow x^2-px-p+c \)
  \(\;\;here;\)
\(\quad a=1,\quad b=-p,\quad c=c-p \)

\(If\) \(\alpha\) \(and\) \(\beta\) \(are\) \(the\) \(zeroes,\) \(then\)
\(\qquad \alpha+\beta = -\dfrac{b}{a} = p \)
\(\;\;and\)
\(\qquad \alpha\beta = \dfrac{c-p}{1} = c-p \)

\(\;\;Now,\)
\(\qquad (\alpha+1)(\beta+1)=0 \)
\(\quad\Rightarrow \alpha\beta+\alpha+\beta+1=0 \)
\(\;\;Substituting \;values,\)
\(\qquad\;\; (c-p)+p+1=0 \)
\(\quad\quad\Rightarrow c+1=0 \)
\(\quad\quad\Rightarrow c=-1 \)

\(\;\therefore The \;correct \;option \;is\;(iii)-1\)

5. If \( a - b, a \) and \( a + b \) are the zeroes of the cubic polynomial \( p(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 - 6x + 8\), then the value of \( a \) and \( b \) are
 \( (i) \quad a = 1 \quad (ii) \quad b = \pm 3 \\ (iii) \quad a = -1\quad (iv) \quad b = a \)

Solution: The correct options are \((i)\) and \((ii) \)


Explanation:

\(\quad Given,\)

\(\qquad\; p(x)=x^3-3x^2-6x+8 \)
\(\;\;\;The \;zeroes\; are\)
\(\qquad\; a-b,\quad a,\quad a+b \)
\(\;\;For \;a \;cubic\; polynomial,\)
\(\qquad\; \alpha+\beta+\gamma = \dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow (a-b)+a+(a+b) = -\left(\dfrac{-3}{1}\right) \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow 3a=3 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow a=1 \)
\(Also, \)
\(\qquad\quad \alpha\beta\gamma = \dfrac{-d}{a} \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow (a-b)(a)(a+b) = \dfrac{-8}{1} \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow a(a^2-b^2) = -8 \)
\(Substituting\) \(a=1,\)
\(\quad\; 1(1^2-b^2) = -8 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow 1-b^2=-8 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow b^2=9 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow b=\pm3 \)

\( \therefore \; a=1 \quad and \quad b=\pm3 \)

\(\therefore The\;correct\;options\) \(are\) \((i)\) \(and\) \((ii) \)

6.  \(Assertion\; (A):\) If \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are the zeroes of the polynomial \( x^2 - 6x + p \) such that \( (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 40 \) then the value of \(p\) is \(-1\).

  \(Reason\; (R):\) The sum and the product of the zeroes of a quadratic polynomial \( ax^2 + bx + c \) are \( \frac{-b}{a} \) and \( \frac{c}{a} \), respectively.

  \(Choose\) \(the\) \(correct\) \(option:\)

  (a) Both assertion \((A)\) and reason \((R)\) are true and R is the correct explanation of assertion \((A)\).
  (b) Both assertion \((A)\) and reason \((R)\) are true but \(R\) is not the correct explanation of assertion \((A)\).
  (c) Assertion \((A)\) is true but reason \((R)\) is false.
  (d) Assertion \((A)\) is false but reason \((R)\) is true.

Solution: \((d)\)Assertion \((A)\) is false but Reason \((R)\) is true


Explanation:

\(\;\;Given,\)
\(\qquad\quad x^2-6x+p \)
\(\;\;If \;\alpha\; and \;\beta\) \(are\) \(the\) \(zeroes,\) \(then\)
\(\qquad \alpha+\beta = -\dfrac{b}{a} = -\dfrac{-6}{1} = 6 \)
\(\;\;and\)

\(\;\;Now,\)
\(\quad\; (\alpha+\beta)^2-2\alpha\beta=40 \)
\(\;\;Substituting \;values,\)
\(\quad\; (6)^2-2(p)=40 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow 36-2p=40 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow -2p=4 \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow p=-2 \)

\(Hence, \;Assertion \;(A) \;is \;false\).


\(Reason\; (R):\)

\(\qquad \alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(This\; statement \;is \;true.\)


\( \therefore \) \((d)\;Assertion\; (A) \;is \;false \;but\) \(Reason\) \((R)\) \(is\) \(true\)

10. If one of the zeroes of the polynomial \( x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c \) is -1, then the product of the other two zeroes is

  \(\;(a)\) \( b - a + 1\) \(\quad (b)\) \( b - a - 1\)

\(\qquad \alpha\beta(-1) = \dfrac{-c}{1} \)

\(\;\;\Rightarrow \alpha\beta = 1-a+b \)
\(\;\;\Rightarrow \alpha\beta = b-a+1 \)

\( \therefore \alpha\beta=b-a+1 \;is \;the\) \(correct\) \(answer \)

11. \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are the zeroes of the polynomial \( x^2 - 6x + a \). If \( 3\alpha + 2\beta = 20 \), then the value of \( a \) is
  \((a)\) 5 \( \quad(b)\) -7 \( \quad(c)\) 12 \( \quad(d)\) -16

Solution: \((d)\;-16\) \(is \;the \;correct\) \(option.\)


Explanation:

   \(\;\;Given\; polynomial,\)
\(\qquad\quad\quad\quad x^2-6x+a \)

\(\qquad\quad \alpha+\beta = -\left(\dfrac{-6}{1}\right) = 6 \)
\(\;\;and\)
\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha\beta = \dfrac{a}{1} = a \)

\(\;\;\;Given,\)
\(\qquad\quad\quad 3\alpha+2\beta=20 \)
\(\;\;Also,\)
\(\qquad\quad\quad \alpha+\beta=6 \)
\(\quad\quad\Rightarrow 2(\alpha+\beta)=2\times 6 \)
\(\quad\quad\Rightarrow 2\alpha+2\beta=12 \)
\(\;Subtracting,\)
\( (3\alpha+2\beta)-(2\alpha+2\beta) = 20-12 \)

12. If \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are the zeroes of the polynomial \( 2x^2 - 5x + 7 \), then find another polynomial whose zeroes are \( 2\alpha + 3\beta \) and \( 3\alpha + 2\beta \)

Solution:

\(\;\;Given \;polynomial,\)
\(\quad\quad\quad\quad 2x^2-5x+7 \)
   \(\;\;If \;\alpha\; and \;\beta\; are\; the\) \(zeroes,\) \(then\)
\(\quad\quad \alpha+\beta = -\left(-\dfrac52\right) = \dfrac52 \)
\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha\beta = \dfrac72 \)

\(\;New \;zeroes\; are\; 2\alpha+3\beta \; and\; 3\alpha+2\beta \)

   \(Sum \;of\; new\; zeroes:\)
\(\quad (2\alpha+3\beta)+(3\alpha+2\beta) \)
\(\quad\quad =5\alpha+5\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =5(\alpha+\beta) \)
\(\quad\quad =5\left(\dfrac52\right) \)
\(\quad\quad =\dfrac{25}{2} \)

   \(Product \;of \;new\; zeroes:\)
\(\quad\quad (2\alpha+3\beta)(3\alpha+2\beta) \)
\(\quad =6\alpha^2+13\alpha\beta+6\beta^2 \)
\(\quad =6\alpha^2+12\alpha\beta+6\beta^2+\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad =6(\alpha^2+2\alpha\beta+\beta^2)+\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad =6(\alpha+\beta)^2+\alpha\beta \)

\(\quad =6\left(\dfrac52\right)^2+\dfrac72 \)


\(\quad =6\left(\dfrac{25}{4}\right)+\dfrac72 \)


\(\quad =3\left(\dfrac{25}{2}\right)+\dfrac72 \)


\(\quad =\dfrac{75}{2}+\dfrac72 \)


\(\qquad\; =\dfrac{82}{2} \)
\(\qquad\; =41 \)

\(\therefore \;Required \;new\; polynomial\; is\; in\) \(the\) \(form,\)
\(\quad p(x)=k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta] \)
\(\quad\quad =2\left[x^2-\dfrac{25}{2}x+41\right] \)
\(\quad\quad =2x^2-25x+82\;\;where\; k=2. \)

13. If \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are the zeroes of the polynomial \( ax^2 + bx + c \), then find the value of the following:

\((i)\) \( \alpha^2 + \beta^2 \) \(\quad(ii)\) \( \alpha^2 + \alpha \beta + \beta^3 \)
\((iii)\) \( \alpha^2 \beta + \alpha\beta^2 \) \(\quad(iv)\) \( \alpha - \beta \)
\((v)\) \( \alpha^2 + \beta^2 - \alpha \beta \).

Solution:

     \(Given,\)

\(\quad\quad\quad ax^2+bx+c \)
    \(If \;\alpha\; and \;\beta\; are\; the\) \(zeroes,\) \(then\)
\(\quad\quad \alpha+\beta=\dfrac{-b}{a} \)
\(\;and\)
\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha\beta=\dfrac{c}{a} \)

\(\quad(i)\)

\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha^2+\beta^2 \)
\(\quad\quad =\alpha^2+\beta^2+2\alpha\beta-2\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =(\alpha+\beta)^2-2\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =\left(-\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^2-2\left(\dfrac{c}{a}\right) \)
\(\quad\quad =\dfrac{b^2-2ac}{a^2} \)

\(\quad(ii)\)

\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha^2+\alpha\beta+\beta^2 \)
\(\quad\quad =\alpha^2+\alpha\beta+\beta^2+\alpha\beta-\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =\alpha^2+2\alpha\beta+\beta^2-\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =(\alpha+\beta)^2-\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =\left(-\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^2-\dfrac{c}{a} \)
\(\quad\quad =\dfrac{b^2-ac}{a^2} \)

\(\quad(iii)\)

\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha^2\beta+\alpha\beta^2 \)
\(\quad\quad =\alpha\beta(\alpha+\beta) \)
\(\quad\quad =\dfrac{c}{a}\left(-\dfrac{b}{a}\right) \)
\(\quad\quad =-\dfrac{bc}{a^2} \)

\(\quad(iv)\)

\(\;\;We\;know\;that,\)
\(\quad (\alpha-\beta)^2 \) \( =\alpha^2-2\alpha\beta+\beta^2 \)

\(\Rightarrow (\alpha-\beta)^2 =\alpha^2-2\alpha\beta+\beta^2+2\alpha\beta-2\alpha\beta \)
\(\Rightarrow (\alpha-\beta)^2 =\alpha^2+2\alpha\beta+\beta^2-2\alpha\beta-2\alpha\beta \)

\(\;\Rightarrow (\alpha-\beta)^2 =(\alpha+\beta)^2-4\alpha\beta \)
\(\;\Rightarrow (\alpha-\beta)^2 =\left(-\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^2-4\left(\dfrac{c}{a}\right) \)
\(\;\Rightarrow (\alpha-\beta)^2 \) \( =\dfrac{b^2-4ac}{a^2} \)

\(\;\Rightarrow \alpha-\beta = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{a} \)

\(\quad(v)\)

\(\quad\quad\quad \alpha^2+\beta^2-\alpha\beta \)

\(\quad\quad =\alpha^2+\beta^2+2\alpha\beta-2\alpha\beta-\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =(\alpha+\beta)^2-3\alpha\beta \)
\(\quad\quad =\left(\dfrac{-b}{a}\right)^2-3\left(\dfrac{c}{a}\right) \)
\(\quad\quad =\dfrac{b^2-3ac}{a^2} \)

NCERT Class 10 Math Chapter 2 – Polynomials FAQs

Clear conceptual answers to the most common queries from Polynomials.

What is a polynomial?

A polynomial is an algebraic expression with variables and coefficients, using only non‑negative integer exponents. Example: \( 3x^2 + 2x - 1 \).

How are zeroes of a quadratic polynomial related to coefficients?

For \( ax^2+bx+c \), sum of zeroes = \( -b/a \), product = \( c/a \).

What is the Division Algorithm for polynomials?

If \( p(x) \) and \( g(x) \) are polynomials with \( g(x) \neq 0 \), then there exist polynomials \( q(x) \) and \( r(x) \) such that \( p(x) = g(x) q(x) + r(x) \), where \( r(x)=0 \) or degree of \( r(x) < \) degree of \( g(x) \).

How to find zeroes of a quadratic polynomial?

Factorise or use the quadratic formula: \( x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a} \).

What is the geometric meaning of zeroes?

The zeroes are the x‑coordinates where the graph \( y = p(x) \) intersects the x‑axis.

Can a cubic polynomial have only one real zero?

Yes, a cubic polynomial always has at least one real zero, and it can have exactly one real zero if the other two are complex.

How do you verify relationship between zeroes and coefficients?

Find zeroes, then compute sum, sum of pairwise products, and product; compare with \( -b/a, c/a, -d/a \) etc.

What does it mean if a polynomial has no real zero?

It means the graph does not touch or cross the x‑axis. For a quadratic, discriminant < 0.

Is zero a polynomial?

Yes, the zero polynomial is a constant polynomial with all coefficients equal to zero. Its degree is undefined.

What is the degree of a constant polynomial?

The degree of a non‑zero constant polynomial is 0. The zero polynomial has no defined degree.
