Solution:

\(\quad\)\(Let\;D\; be\; the \;midpoint\; of\; AB\) \(of\) \(\triangle ABC,\) \(where\) \(DE\parallel BC,\) \(meeting\) \(AC\) \(at\) \(E\).


\(\quad\)\(To \;prove:\)

\(\qquad\qquad E\;is\; the \;midpoint\; of\) \(AC,\)
\(\qquad\qquad\quad i.e.,\) \(AE=EC\).

\(\;\;\)\(Proof:\)

\(\qquad\)\(Since\;D \;is \;the\; midpoint \;of\) \(AB\),

\(\qquad\quad\dfrac{AE}{EC}=1\)

\(\qquad\Rightarrow AE=EC\)

\(\quad\;\therefore\) \(E\;is \;the \;midpoint \;of \;AC\).

Solution:

\(\;\;Given,\)

\(\qquad\;ABCD \;is \;a \;trapezium\) \(in\) \(which\) \(AB\) \(||\) \(DC\) \(and\) \(its\) \(diagonals\) \(intersect\) \(each\) \(other\) \(at\) \(the\) \(point\) \(O\).


\(\quad To \;Prove:\) \(\quad\;\dfrac{AO}{BO}=\dfrac{CO}{DO}\)


\(\;Construction:\)
\(\qquad\quad EO\parallel DC, \;meeting\) \(AD\) \(at\) \(E\) \(is\) \(drawn.\)

\(\quad\)\(Proof:\)

\(\qquad\quad \;Since \;in \;\triangle DAB,\;EO\parallel AB,\)

\(\qquad\)\(By \;the\; Basic\) \(Proportionality\) \(Theorem,\)

\(\qquad\quad\dfrac{DO}{OB}=\dfrac{DE}{EA}\qquad\ldots(1)\)


\(\qquad\;\;\)\(Also, \;in \;\triangle DAC,\;EO\parallel DC,\)
\(\qquad\)\(By \;the \;Basic\) \(Proportionality\) \(Theorem,\)

\(\qquad\qquad\qquad\quad Hence\;Proved\).

NCERT Class 10 Math Chapter 6 – Triangles FAQs

Clear conceptual answers to the most common queries from Triangles.

What is the Basic Proportionality Theorem?

If a line is parallel to one side of a triangle and intersects the other two sides, it divides those sides in the same ratio. Also known as Thales theorem.

What are the similarity criteria for triangles?

AAA (or AA), SSS, and SAS. If any of these conditions hold, the triangles are similar.

How is Pythagoras theorem proved using similarity?

Draw a perpendicular from the right angle to the hypotenuse; the resulting smaller triangles are similar to the original and to each other. Use proportions to derive AB²+BC²=AC².

What is the ratio of areas of similar triangles?

The ratio of areas is equal to the square of the ratio of corresponding sides.

What is the altitude‑on‑hypotenuse theorem?

In a right triangle, the altitude to the hypotenuse is the geometric mean of the two segments of the hypotenuse. Each leg is the geometric mean between the hypotenuse and the segment adjacent to the leg.

How to prove two right triangles are similar?

If one acute angle of one right triangle equals one acute angle of the other, they are similar (AA).

What is the converse of Pythagoras theorem?

If the square of one side of a triangle equals the sum of squares of the other two sides, then the triangle is right‑angled.

Is every equilateral triangle similar?

Yes, all equilateral triangles are similar because all angles are 60° (AA criterion).

Can similar triangles be congruent?

Yes, if the scale factor is 1, they are congruent. Congruent triangles are a special case of similar triangles.

What is the application of similarity in real life?

Used in maps, scale models, and indirect measurement (e.g., finding height of a building using shadow lengths).
